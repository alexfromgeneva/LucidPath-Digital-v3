# LucidPath Digital visual direction

- Product mode: `persuade`
- Audience and cadence: Decision-makers and communications leaders at UN agencies, international organizations, NGOs, and public-sector institutions; occasional, high-consideration visits while evaluating specialist partners.
- Visual world (name + the feeling it creates): **International editorial clarity** — composed, evidence-led, globally credible, and human without feeling corporate or institutional.
- Palette family + neutral undertone: Cobalt / sky brand blues with cool white and charcoal neutrals; warm orange appears only as a directional accent.
- Type treatment: Sans-first Inter hierarchy with oversized, tightly tracked display headlines and highly readable body copy.
- Composition: Persuasive editorial document with generous whitespace, asymmetric split sections, concise proof points, and one crafted path motif.
- Shape language: Crisp geometry, quiet 6–8px corners, fine rules, restrained pills only for compact metadata, and minimal shadow.
- Anti-references (defaults this app must not drift toward): Generic SaaS dashboards, purple gradients, glassmorphism, floating-card mosaics, emoji iconography, stock-photo corporate teams, oversized rounded containers, vague claims, decorative badges, and dense explanatory chrome.

## Surface contract

`persuade / global-institution decision-makers / understand Alex’s expertise and evidence / start a project`

## Brand foundations

- Deep Blue `#1F4788`: primary brand fields, calls to action, and major headings.
- Ocean Blue `#2E5C8A`: secondary emphasis and hover states.
- Sky Blue `#4A7BA7`: links, line icons, and secondary accents.
- Light Blue `#E8F0F7`: calm section backgrounds and subtle data surfaces.
- Warm Orange `#F7931E`: sparing highlights and path endpoints.
- Charcoal `#333333`: body copy and the featured-work/footer field.
- Inter is the primary digital typeface; body text remains at least 16px with 1.5 line height.
- Interactive targets are at least 44px, keyboard focus is always visible, and motion is disabled when reduced motion is requested.
